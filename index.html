About the studio

Where every child gets to be the star of their own story.

Hyderabad Kids Studio – Vizag Baby Studio is a theme-based studio in Visakhapatnam designed around one idea: kids should enjoy their photoshoot. We build imaginative sets, keep the studio clean and calm, and take our time — so your little one is relaxed and the pictures look natural.

From first birthdays to everyday milestones, families trust us for the patience, punctuality and attention to detail that go into every frame.

Questions, answered

Ask on WhatsApp
How do I book a shoot?
Send us a message on WhatsApp (79954 33705) or call the studio. Share your preferred theme, your child's age and a date, and we'll confirm the slot. You can also use the booking form on this page.
How many themes do you have?
We have 30+ curated themes — from florals and cartoons to seaside, vintage and more. Browse our Instagram (@hyderabadkidsstudiovizag) for the latest sets.
What ages do you photograph?
Kids of all ages are welcome. Tell us your child's age when you book and we'll suggest themes and outfits that suit them best.
Are costumes and props provided?
Our themes come with props and costumes designed for each set. Ask us what's available for your chosen theme when you book.
What are your prices?
Pricing depends on the theme and package you choose. Message or call us for current rates — we'll happily help you pick something that fits your budget.
Is the studio available on rental?
Yes, we're a theme-based rental studio. Contact us with your requirements and preferred time and we'll let you know the availability.
Where exactly are you located?
